
# Game Design — Game 1

## 1. Overview

An original action RPG featuring supernatural encounters, combat, character progression, and a narrative-driven world.

The game is currently in early development. This document records established gameplay concepts and mechanics and may evolve as development progresses.

## 2. Core Stats

| Stat | Description |
|---|---|
| HP | Health. Reaching zero normally means defeat, although certain mechanics may alter this. |
| XP | Experience gained through progression. |
| STR | Strength-related stat. |
| AGL | Agility-related stat. |
| DEF | Defensive stat. |
| DEX | Dexterity-related stat. |
| AUR | Aura. A stat primarily intended for aura-related progression and interactions. |
| END | Endurance. Influences survivability and the ability to withstand lethal damage. |
| EN | Energy used to activate abilities. |
| WP | World Power. Currency used to unlock abilities. |
| MALICE | A universal resource associated with the protagonist's supernatural abilities. |

## 3. Endurance (END)

END influences survivability.

One established concept is that certain lethal hits may leave the player at 1 HP rather than immediately defeating them.

The exact conditions, limitations and balancing of this mechanic remain undecided.

## 4. Energy (EN)

Energy is the resource used to activate abilities.

Its regeneration, costs and limitations are yet to be defined.

## 5. World Power (WP)

World Power is a progression currency used to unlock abilities.

Its acquisition and spending mechanics are yet to be defined.

## 6. Malice

Malice is a universal resource associated with the protagonist's supernatural abilities.

Malice originates from the protagonist and is channelled outward through supernatural abilities. It serves as a shared resource across personalities, with combat and ability interactions contributing to its generation.

- Damage dealt in combat contributes to Malice generation.
- Mixing abilities from different personalities increases Malice generation.
- Malice is used in the supernatural ability system.
- Personality determines the abilities available, while Malice serves as a shared resource.

Exact regeneration values, limits and mechanics remain undecided.

## 7. Ability & Synchronization System

Abilities depend on the personality currently being used.

Each personality grants access to its own set of abilities, with power drawn from the supernatural world into the real world through synchronization.

- **Personality-dependent abilities:** Each personality has its own abilities and fighting style.
- **Synchronization:** The degree of synchronization between the real world and the supernatural world influences how much power can be drawn into reality.
- **Power scaling:** Higher synchronization allows greater access to the power associated with the active personality.

## 8. World Corruption & Encounter Scaling

As the real world becomes increasingly corrupted by the supernatural world:

- More enemies appear.
- Mini-boss encounters become more frequent.
- Bosses become more powerful.
- More powerful entities may become accessible.

World corruption influences the difficulty and scale of encounters.

## 9. World Power & Optional Boss Progression

World Power can be spent to unlock abilities, but spending it is optional.

The player can choose between a relatively minimal progression path or spending their WP to unlock more abilities, potentially increasing the range of tools available for the challenges ahead.

| Approach | Potential encounters |
|---|---|
| Minimal spending | Around 2–3 mini-bosses and the final boss |
| Full spending | Around 10 mini-bosses, 2 additional major bosses and 2 secret bosses |

These are proposed encounter counts and may change during development.

## 10. Development Status

This document describes concepts established during early development. Details may change as mechanics are implemented and tested.
